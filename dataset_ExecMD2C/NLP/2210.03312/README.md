## Reproduction task

The method embeds distillation-resistant watermarks into NLP models to protect ownership. In `template.py`, complete `softmax_signal_wm`, `KLLoss.forward`, and the other TODO-marked functions to reproduce DRW's soft-label watermark signal injection and reproduce the soft-label cross-entropy loss used for distillation.
