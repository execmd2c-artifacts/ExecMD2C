## Reproduction task

EDMP plans robot motions with diffusion guided by an ensemble of costs. In `template.py`, complete `ResidualConvolutionBlock.forward`, `TemporalUNet.forward`, and the other TODO-marked functions to apply a time-conditioned residual convolution block over a trajectory sequence and predict diffusion noise for a trajectory with the full temporal U-Net denoiser.
