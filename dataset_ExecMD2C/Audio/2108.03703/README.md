## Reproduction task

The autoencoder reconstructs high-quality audio spectra from long, lossy audio sequences with low latency. In `template.py`, complete `SingleBlock.call` and `create_model` to apply one spectral enhancement convolution block and assemble the spectral enhancement autoencoder model.
