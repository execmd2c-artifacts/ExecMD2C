## Reproduction task

TalkNet 2 synthesizes speech spectrograms from text with explicit duration and pitch predictions. In `template.py`, complete `merge`, `TalkNet2.forward`, and the other TODO-marked functions to merge a list of variable-length tensors into one padded batch tensor and generate mel spectrograms from text, durations, and explicit pitch.
