In `template.py`, complete `GraphAttentionLayer._pairwise_mul_nodes`, `RawGAT_ST.forward`, and the other TODO-marked functions to reproduce pairwise multiplicative node-relation construction and reproduce the full RawGAT-ST multiplicative-fusion forward pass.
