## Reproduction task

MusicMagus edits generated music from text instructions without task-specific training. In `template.py`, complete `prepare_unet`, `AudioLDM2Pipeline.auto_corr_loss`, and the other TODO-marked functions to reproduce MusicMagus/Pix2Pix-Zero UNet attention-processor preparation and reproduce the latent-noise autocorrelation regularizer.
