## Reproduction task

MuseMorphose transfers full-song piano style with a fine-grained transformer VAE. In `template.py`, complete `VAETransformerEncoder.forward`, `MuseMorphose.forward`, and the other TODO-marked functions to reproduce the VAE Transformer encoder forward pass and reproduce the full MuseMorphose Transformer-VAE forward pass.
