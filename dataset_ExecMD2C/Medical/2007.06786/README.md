## Reproduction task

Meta-rPPG estimates heart rate remotely from video with transductive meta-learning. In `template.py`, complete `Synthetic_Gradient_Generator.forward`, `ordLoss.forward`, and the other TODO-marked functions to generate synthetic gradients for Meta-rPPG adaptation and compute the Meta-rPPG ordinal negative log-likelihood.
