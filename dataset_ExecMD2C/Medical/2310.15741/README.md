## Reproduction task

The model classifies medical images with interpretable prototypes and privileged information. In `template.py`, complete `squash`, `sep_loss`, and the other TODO-marked functions to apply capsule vector squashing along one capsule dimension and penalize samples that are too close to prototypes from other attribute bins.
